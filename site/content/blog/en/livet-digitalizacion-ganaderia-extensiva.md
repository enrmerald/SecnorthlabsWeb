Extensive livestock farming is an essential activity for the land, for food production and for the conservation of rural areas. It still lives with a complex reality, though: dispersed holdings, patchy connectivity, manual records, growing administrative demands and mounting pressure on margins. Technology can help solve part of these challenges, but only when it is designed from the ground up with the people who will use it every day in mind.

That conviction is where **LIVET** comes from: [Ewala](https://ewala.es)'s platform for the intelligent digitalisation of extensive livestock farming. It is a solution conceived to improve operational management, strengthen traceability, support compliance and provide better information for taking economic and production decisions with sounder judgement.

The project is currently being developed within the European **[GUARDIANS](https://ewala.es/#guardians)** programme, with an approach based on co-creation alongside local farmers, on real usefulness and on security by design.

## Extensive livestock farming: an essential sector that needs better-adapted tools

Running an extensive holding means coordinating a lot of information at once. Animals, plots, treatments, reproductive status, health alerts, movements, purchases, sales, suppliers and documentation all coexist in the daily work of an activity that, in many cases, still depends on physical records, repetitive tasks or tools that do not talk to each other.

That fragmentation wastes time, increases the risk of error and makes it hard to get a complete view of the holding. The problem is sharper in rural or mountain environments, where connectivity is not always guaranteed and where travelling to resolve an issue or check a piece of data has a real cost.

There is also a key question on top of that: profitability. The way costs evolve, purchasing decisions, batch management, supplier relationships and health or reproductive planning all have a direct impact on the competitiveness of the holding.

Digitalising cannot be limited to replacing paper with screens; it has to help people work better and decide better.

## What LIVET is

**LIVET — Livestock Innovation and Virtual Ecosystem for Tracking —** is a modular digital platform aimed at modernising the management of extensive livestock farming.

It combines:

- A web platform for analysis, planning and control.
- An offline-first mobile application designed for work in the field.
- An optional integration layer with [IoT devices](/en/blog/dispositivos-iot-y-las-capas-de-la-ciberseguridad/), such as GPS collars or calving sensors.
- Traceability, operational tracking and economic analytics features.

LIVET centralises critical information about the holding in a single environment: animal history, reproductive and health status, treatments, alerts, purchases and sales, suppliers, plots, inventory and economic metrics. It also includes multi-user management, action logging and an architecture ready to grow gradually.

The goal is clear: turning scattered information into a practical tool for management and decision support.

## A solution born in the field

LIVET did not start as a closed technology proposal, but as a platform built from real needs identified on livestock holdings. That way of working has been decisive in prioritising useful features and avoiding development detached from daily operations.

Among LIVET's most relevant capabilities:

- **Scanning ear tags with a phone**, to reduce errors and speed up animal identification.
- **Offline operation**, with later synchronisation when coverage returns.
- **SIGPAC integration**, to provide territorial context and support plot management.
- **Reproductive and health control**, with event tracking and alerts.
- **Economic analytics by batch, animal and supplier**, aimed at improving visibility over profitability.
- **Optional, modular IoT integration**, adding sensors where they bring value without turning them into a barrier to adoption.

This approach lets LIVET be ambitious and realistic at the same time: useful from early stages, adaptable to different degrees of digitalisation and designed to evolve with the market.

## A pioneering proposal

The market has solutions focused on specific features, such as livestock location, virtual fencing or spot monitoring of certain indicators. LIVET puts forward a broader vision: an end-to-end management and decision layer for extensive livestock farming.

Its differential proposal rests on five pillars.

### 1. Operational digitalisation with real impact

LIVET reduces manual tasks, improves how information is organised and supports clearer traceability of daily activity.

### 2. Offline-first design

Limited connectivity is not treated as an exception but as a normal condition in many rural environments. The application is built to keep working in the field and synchronise afterwards.

### 3. Integrated economic analytics

The platform does not only help manage animals and processes, but also to understand the economic dimension of the holding better.

### 4. Optional, modular IoT

Technology is added proportionately. LIVET can integrate with connected devices, but it does not depend on a rigid architecture or an expensive rollout in order to add value.

### 5. Cybersecurity and data control by design

At Ewala they understand that digitalising the agricultural and livestock sector has to be built on solid foundations of security, traceability and data sovereignty. The information of a holding has strategic value, which is why LIVET builds that view into its architecture.

Security is not a later addition, but part of the product proposal.

## GUARDIANS and the Test Before Invest opportunity

LIVET is being developed thanks to **[Open Call #1 – Co-Creation](https://guardians-project.eu/call/open-call-1-co-creation/)** of the European [GUARDIANS](https://guardians-project.eu/) project, which supports digital solutions for the agricultural and livestock sector built together with their real users.

That framework has made it possible to move from the initial product definition to a solution with clear requirements, a validated architecture and a market-oriented roadmap.

A new phase now opens with **[Open Call #2 – Test Before Invest](https://guardians-project.eu/call/open-call-2-test-before-invest/)**, aimed at having new holdings test and validate the technologies developed. In LIVET's case, this opportunity is aimed at extensive livestock holdings interested in trying a digital management tool adapted to their reality.

## A commitment to rural areas

[Ewala](https://ewala.es) is an Asturian technology company specialising in cybersecurity, applied innovation and the development of its own solutions. Its R&D department works to turn technical knowledge into products that respond to concrete challenges in the market and in society.

The commitment to LIVET is part of that vision. Rural areas need technology designed to fit them: robust, accessible, secure and built to generate value. Backing livestock digitalisation is also backing the competitiveness of the region, the efficient use of resources and new opportunities for economic development from Asturias.

LIVET brings that philosophy into a concrete product: a solution that reduces friction, improves traceability, supports more efficient management and puts technology at the service of the people who sustain an essential part of the productive fabric.

## Technology born in Asturias, looking further afield

With LIVET the aim is to help extensive livestock farming get digital tools equal to its challenges. Tools that simplify the work, reinforce professional judgement and bring visibility where information used to be fragmented.

That is the path being built: less paper, more traceability; less uncertainty, more information; fewer decisions taken blind, more control over the holding.

LIVET was born in Asturias, but it is meant to grow. To grow with the sector, with new holdings, with new features and with a clear ambition: becoming a reference in the secure digitalisation of extensive livestock farming.
