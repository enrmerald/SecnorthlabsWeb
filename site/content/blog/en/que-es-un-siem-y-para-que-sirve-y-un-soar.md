## What is a SIEM?

A **SIEM** is a platform for managing information security by collecting, analysing and correlating security events in real time. Its main job is to provide **visibility and control** over an organisation's technology estate, detecting suspicious or unusual activity on the network.

### Did you know a SIEM combines two earlier technologies?

- **SEM (Security Event Management):** monitors security events in real time and responds immediately to suspicious incidents.
- **SIM (Security Information Management):** focuses on the collection, storage and long-term analysis of security data.

Bringing the two together produces the **SIEM**, which does not just log events: it also detects attack patterns and can trigger **automated responses** to mitigate risk quickly.

## How does a SIEM work?

A **SIEM** works through several key stages that turn data into actionable security intelligence:

1. **Data collection:** pulls information from multiple sources, such as firewalls, antivirus systems, applications and network devices.
2. **Data normalisation:** converts the data into a unified format, making analysis and correlation possible.
3. **Event correlation:** identifies patterns and relationships between different events to detect potential threats.
4. **Alerting:** issues real-time notifications or runs automatic responses when security incidents are detected.
5. **Logging and audit:** stores events for forensic analysis, compliance audits and continuous security improvement.

## The role of SIEM in a complete SOC

A **SIEM** is the heart of a **SOC** (*Security Operations Centre*), because it provides the foundation for **continuous security monitoring**. The current cybersecurity landscape, though, demands more than visibility and alerts.

That is where **SOAR** (*Security Orchestration, Automation and Response*) comes in.

## What is SOAR and how does it complement a SIEM?

A **SOAR** is a platform that **automates and orchestrates** responses to security incidents. While the **SIEM** identifies and alerts on threats, the **SOAR** makes it possible to:

- **Automate repetitive tasks**, such as collecting data for incident analysis.
- **Orchestrate complex workflows**, integrating multiple security tools.
- **Respond proactively and quickly** to threats, minimising the impact on the organisation.

## SIEM + SOAR = an efficient, resilient SOC

Together, **SIEM** and **SOAR** create a robust cybersecurity ecosystem:

- **SIEM:** detects, analyses and alerts on incidents.
- **SOAR:** automates responses, coordinates teams and cuts reaction times.
