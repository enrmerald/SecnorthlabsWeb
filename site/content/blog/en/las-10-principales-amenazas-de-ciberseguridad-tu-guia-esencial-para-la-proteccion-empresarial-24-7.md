In a world where cyberattacks never rest, continuous monitoring has become a fundamental requirement. Managed cybersecurity services, backed by a SOC (Security Operations Centre), provide the constant watch your company needs. Perimeter and endpoint protection is no longer an option, it is a necessity.

### The most dangerous threats of 2024

The current threat landscape shows increasingly sophisticated attack patterns. The main threats include:

#### 1. SocGholish: a wolf in sheep's clothing

This malware, present in 42% of detections, disguises itself as a legitimate browser update. Only 24/7 monitoring can detect and neutralise these threats before it is too late.

#### 2. New emerging threats

LandUpdate808 and ClearFake have burst onto the cybersecurity scene. These new attack patterns show why protecting hybrid infrastructure is crucial in the current environment.

### How to protect your company: MDR for small and medium businesses

Implementing an MDR (Managed Detection and Response) service is fundamental for smaller companies looking for robust protection. A managed MDR service offers:

- Continuous 24/7 monitoring
- Endpoint protection
- Immediate incident response
- Analysis of attack and defence patterns

### Artificial intelligence in cybersecurity

LLMs and artificial intelligence are transforming how we detect and respond to threats. An AI-powered SIEM/XDR for smaller companies can:

- Identify unknown attack patterns
- Automate security responses
- Improve perimeter protection
- Reduce false positives

### Sensing and monitoring: the power of prevention

The key to effective defence is early detection. Modern event monitoring systems use advanced sensing to:

- Detect anomalous behaviour
- Identify vulnerabilities before they are exploited
- Keep a continuous record of system activity
- Make vulnerability patching easier

### Top 10 malware: what you should know

1. SocGholish: malware disguised as legitimate updates
2. LandUpdate808: a new threat that installs remote control tooling
3. ClearFake: distributes malicious software through compromised websites
4. ZPHP: a variant spread through fake updates
5. Agent Tesla: steals credentials and logs system activity
6. CoinMiner: uses your resources for cryptocurrency mining
7. Arechclient2: an advanced trojan that steals sensitive data
8. Mirai: attacks IoT devices to build botnets
9. NanoCore: a malicious remote access tool
10. Lumma Stealer: steals banking and personal information

### Conclusion: your action plan

Cybersecurity is no longer optional for modern companies. A well-implemented SOC, together with 24/7 MDR services, can be the difference between safety and disaster. Do you want to protect your company with the best cybersecurity practices?
