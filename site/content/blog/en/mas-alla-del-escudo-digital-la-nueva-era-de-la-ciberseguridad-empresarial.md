## How enterprise cybersecurity has evolved

Digital threats evolve constantly. A recent Microsoft case, in which 240 malicious domains were seized, shows that companies need protection beyond traditional antivirus.

### From antivirus to MDR: the new era of digital protection

#### What antivirus is, and why it is no longer enough

- It detects known malware by comparing it against a database of previous threats

- Reactive, limited protection

- It does not identify new threats or suspicious behaviour

#### EDR: the next level in security

- **Real-time behaviour** analysis.
- **Proactive** threat detection.
- Full endpoint visibility (any device connected to the network).
- Identification of suspicious patterns, even in advanced threats.

#### MDR: the complete answer for companies

- Combines EDR technology with cybersecurity experts.
- Continuous **24/7** monitoring.
- Immediate response to critical incidents.
- Protection against advanced threats such as ransomware and targeted attacks.

### What MDR brings to your company

1. **Proactive protection**: early threat detection

2. **Expert team**: professional response without building it in-house

3. **Continuous monitoring**: 24/7 watch over your infrastructure

4. **Immediate response**: threats neutralised in real time

### Why implement MDR now?

Cyberattacks keep getting more sophisticated. The recent ONNX case, for instance, shows attackers using advanced techniques such as:

- **Bypassing 2FA authentication.**
- **Phishing with QR codes.**
- Use of **encrypted JavaScript.**
- Hosting on "bulletproof" services.

### **Conclusion**

Digital security is no longer optional. **MDR** provides the end-to-end protection your company needs, combining advanced technology with human expertise to keep your systems safe.
