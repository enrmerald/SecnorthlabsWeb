### What it is and how it works

At heart, it is an innovative security solution whose job is to **integrate and correlate data** from multiple sources.
XDR is a detection and response solution that goes beyond the limited scope of EDR, which only protects endpoints, and of SIEM, which focuses on collecting and correlating events without automated response.

### The main characteristics of XDR

Here are the main characteristics of XDR.

- **Advanced threat detection:** using artificial intelligence and other systems, which lets it spot attacks.
- **Automation:** triggers automatic or semi-automatic responses that mitigate risk once something is detected.
- **Fewer false positives:** the advanced analysis XDR performs reduces the number of false alerts.
- **Multiple security layers:** delivers complete protection by unifying data from networks, email, servers, cloud workloads and endpoints.

### IT security with XDR

If we talk about IT security and what XDR means within it, the key point is that it represents the evolution of conventional detection and response solutions.
It extends **protection to an organisation's entire digital infrastructure**.
So, answering **what XDR is in IT security**: it is a modern technology whose goal is to detect, analyse and neutralise attacks across every layer of the security stack, providing a solid defence.
Today threats can appear through many entry points: email carrying malware, cloud breaches, connected devices and much more.
XDR technology correlates events from different platforms in order to detect suspicious patterns.
In other words, **it gets ahead of the attack** before it causes serious harm.

### XDR & SIEM/EDR

We mentioned earlier that **XDR software** has clear differences from other security tools.
Although they share the same goal — protecting IT systems — the distinguishing factors are worth underlining.
Unlike an EDR, which works in isolation on individual devices, XDR integrates information from multiple layers such as networks, servers, identities and cloud environments, which allows more effective detection of advanced attacks.
And while a SIEM depends on rules and retrospective analysis, XDR applies artificial intelligence and automation to respond faster and more precisely to threats.
Read more in another article on our blog about [XDR vs SIEM](/en/blog/xdr-vs-siem-la-solucion-para-tu-negocio/).

#### SIEM-XDR

Despite its advanced capability, XDR is usually deployed alongside a SIEM, because the SIEM provides long-term storage, regulatory compliance and a broader view of organisational security. While XDR detects and responds immediately, the SIEM allows deep analysis and correlation of historical events, creating a more complete and effective security strategy.

### Choose XDR software to protect your company

Now that you know **what XDR means**, it is worth knowing why implementing it in your company is advisable.
It brings benefits that improve efficiency in detecting and responding to attacks.

- **Greater visibility**, by monitoring the whole network and not just isolated endpoints.
- **Fast response** to attacks, automated and without manual intervention.
- **Fewer alerts** related to minor or false incidents.
- **Better use of resources**, by reducing the workload on security teams.
